    Who we are

    A dream that found an address

    BrazaBrazil is the story of two Brazilians who decided to join forces and invest in a project we are proud to call our own.

    Anyone who lives far from Brazil knows what it is to miss a flavour, a family churrasco, or simply walking into a place where you can chat in your own language. Having a shop here in Epsom lets us be part of those small moments in other people’s lives.

    We opened our doors in March 2026 — but our commitment began long before, in every decision, every investment and the responsibility of making it work. Behind the two smiles in the photo there is hard work, worry, learning and plenty of problems solved together. Leciovany and Elionardo are building this story day by day, each taking on responsibilities and learning along the way.

    “We still have a long way to go, but looking at our shop and seeing happy customers coming back already makes us incredibly proud.”

    We want every person to feel welcome and to take home products they are proud to put on their family’s table. That is how we want to grow: caring for quality and respecting everyone who trusts our work.

    To every customer, our team, our families and the friends who support us — thank you. You are part of this achievement.

    With love and gratitude,Leciovany & Elionardo
